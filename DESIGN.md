# Kaminari Creative Studio

Direction comes from the supplied project brief: Dark Editorial × Electric Minimalism × Creative Technology.

ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2. Oversized editorial headlines create the focal point; varied compositions distinguish the studio's capabilities, internal product, and working process. One-time entrance motion gives the hero a deliberate arrival without persistent distraction.

- Near-black and off-white preserve the requested dark editorial identity. Electric blue marks the strike motif and the primary action; pale cyan identifies knowledge connections.
- Space Grotesk gives headlines the specified geometric character. Inter supports longer descriptions and form readability. Fonts are locally bundled.
- A fractured, angled line carries the lightning metaphor without a stock lightning icon. This is an explicitly requested abstract visual, not a proposed final logo.
- The studio name remains a typographic wordmark. The favicon uses its initial.
- The hero places oversized type opposite a restrained wireframe strike. The service index uses full-width rows. The portfolio has one large cover because only one genuine project was supplied. The product section pairs copy with an accessible architecture diagram.
- Thin borders organize editorial sections. No background grid, blurred orb, fake dashboard, decorative status light, or invented social proof.
- Service icons represent workflows, websites, creative systems, and planning. They are secondary to the service names.
- Spacing follows a 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96/128 scale. Large gaps distinguish sections; tighter gaps connect a label and its description.
- Square surfaces and 4px button/input radii preserve precision. A small in-development label communicates actual product status.
- Arrows identify project navigation and the main forward action. Other links use text alone.
- The supplied headline and product copy take precedence over the filter's generic vocabulary preferences. Feature descriptions distinguish development scope from future ideas; none are presented as verified deployed capabilities.
- Contact development mode validates locally and offers a downloadable brief. It never reports delivery without a confirmed endpoint response.
